# Koplowitz–Pasternak family tree

A public family archive transcribed from **Outline Descendant Report for Yeruham Koplowitz**,
the four-page `Koplowitz_Pasternak.pdf` supplied by Hillel. Includes **189 entries across seven generations**:
126 entries in the descendant line and 63 spouse entries (one explicitly unnamed). The 21 October supplement adds 28 sourced Kahan entries, bringing the browsing tree to **217 entries: 148 in the descendant line and 69 spouse entries, across eight generations**.
Repeated names are separate entries unless the source explicitly identifies them as one person.

## Reading the tree

The descendant line starts with Yeruham Koplowitz and an explicitly unknown spouse.
Children are nested under the parent in the descendant line; the other parent is recorded in each child's note,
following placement under the preceding spouse in the report. Spouse order is preserved even where nonchronological.
Each spouse has their own dates, places, marriage details, and page citation.

## Source and uncertainty

All four pages were visually inspected during transcription. PDF metadata identifies Family Tree Maker as the
creator and 23 July 2026 as the creation date. The document does not supply underlying vital-record citations
or identify its compiler. This archive records its claims, not independent confirmation.

- Preserve `Abt`, month-only dates, year-only dates, punctuation, and historical place spellings.
- `NYC`, `Holocaust`, and `Riga Holocaust` in the death field are source wording, not inferred death dates.
- The Hebrew placeholder `הרך הנולד Pasternak` means an unnamed newborn Pasternak; no given name is invented.
- Ada Pasternak's children Boaz and Rami are grouped under Shlomo Norbert Jaschek, not Eitan Jaschek Shilo.
- Max Leo's Joseph and Michael are grouped under Esther Wormser, not Koenigshilfer Martel.
- Amichai's listed children are grouped under Bruria Harari, not Malka Bardugo.
- Ronit Michael's children are grouped under Rael Elk, not Harold Seligman.
- Rosa Koplowitz's Max Warschauer is grouped under Richard Warschauer; Edith and Gerda under Bernard Zimmerman.
- Edith's Bernie Winter is grouped under Winter, not Wolf.
- No gender or current life status has been inferred. Added relatives and shared-household identity matches are explicitly sourced in the 21 October supplement; no unlisted relative is invented.
